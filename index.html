Why Minky

The difference is in the detail

Anti-bacterial protection

Freshness built into selected Mcloth products

Silver anti-bacterial technology is built into selected Mcloth fibres, helping the cloth stay fresher between uses and continue performing beyond 50 washes.

Purpose-built texture

More contact with the mess

Grid weaves, ribbed faces and non-scratch scrub zones are chosen for a reason: lift grease, shift marks or polish a surface without reaching for a one-size-fits-all cloth.

High-density microfibre

Up to 700,000 fibres per cm²

In Mcloth microfibre, density means more fibre meeting the surface. The result is a softer cloth with the absorbency to dry, wipe and polish more effectively.

The Range

A cleaning range with a job for every texture

Choose by task, not by habit: fine microfibre for finishing, absorbent cloths for spills, and the right amount of scrub for kitchen and bathroom grime.
